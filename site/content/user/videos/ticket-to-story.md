A ticket says what someone wants. A plan says what will be touched to get it. This short video shows the Cockpit going from one to the other, using a product list in a small sample shop. It runs about a minute, with captions and no sound.

![Video: from a ticket to a story, in the Cockpit](@video/01-ticket-to-story)

## What you will see

1. Sign in. Only accounts the owner allowed get in. (This recording uses the demo login.)
2. Open the sample shop from the "Open a project" page.
3. Write the ticket in plain words in the **Notes** tab: *Show every product in the catalog as a list with its price and an Add to cart button.*
4. Ask the Cockpit which parts it touches. It makes a guess with a plain text match, and you confirm it.
5. Read the impact: which files and features the change reaches, worked out from your code with no AI model.
6. See the plan it starts you off with, a first read-only step you can edit or add to. Nothing changes until you run the plan and approve the result.
