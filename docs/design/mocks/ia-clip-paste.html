Paste clip: the safe way to bring in a ticket from behind a loginYou are logged in in your own browser. The clipper reads the page there and copies plain data. Nothing is sent to the Cockpit, and no cookie or password ever leaves your browser.

In the Story tab

Paste clip

Paste what the clipper copied.

{ "key": "STORE-142", "title": "Refund a delivered order", "acceptance": ["Refund button...", "..."], "url": "https://acme.atlassian.net/browse/STORE-142" }
Shown as plain text

Key, title, status, description and 5 acceptance lines (S1 to S5). Text is escaped, never rendered as HTML, and no link in it is opened or fetched.

Deterministic 2.1 KB

When it does not work

Template did not match

The site changed its layout, so the saved selectors found nothing for Acceptance criteria.

Clip is too large

Clips over 64 KB are refused. Nothing was saved.

Not a clip

That text is not clipper data. It was not saved.
