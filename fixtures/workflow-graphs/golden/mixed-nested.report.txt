Root
The "root" flow has 5 steps. It starts in *a* and can end in *a › a 2*.

A
  - This flow starts in *a*.
  - *a* is made up of 2 smaller steps (*a › a 1* and *a › a 2*) and begins with *a › a 1*.
  - Anywhere inside it, when "cancel" happens, the flow moves to *other*.
  - When its sub-flow finishes, the flow moves to *b*.
  - On leaving, it runs `cleanUp`.

A › a 1
  - When "next" happens, the flow moves to *a › a 2*.

A › a 2
  - *a › a 2* is an end state — the flow stops there.

B
  - While here, it starts `x`.
  - When "ping" happens, the flow stays in *b* and runs `log`.
  - After 1 second, the flow moves to *c*.
  - Immediately, the flow moves to *c* — only if a custom condition holds.
  - Otherwise, immediately, the flow moves to *a*.
  - When `x` finishes successfully, the flow moves to *c*.
  - If `x` fails, the flow moves to *c* — only if it is fatal.

C
  - When "back" happens, the flow moves to *other*.
  - When "up" happens, the flow moves to *a* — only if it can retry.
  - Otherwise, the flow moves to *b*.

Other
  - There is no way out of *other* — the flow gets stuck there.

Scenarios
  Next, then cancel (gets stuck in other)
    Route: a › a 1 → a › a 2 → other
    Given the flow starts in *a › a 1*
    When "next" happens
    Then the flow moves to *a › a 2*
    And when "cancel" happens
    Then the flow moves to *other*
    And the flow gets stuck in *other*, with no way out

  Next, then on done, then after 1 s, then back (gets stuck in other)
    Route: a › a 1 → a › a 2 → b → c → other
    Given the flow starts in *a › a 1*
    When "next" happens
    Then the flow moves to *a › a 2*
    And when its sub-flow finishes
    Then the flow moves to *b*
    And when 1 second passes
    Then the flow moves to *c*
    And when "back" happens
    Then the flow moves to *other*
    And the flow gets stuck in *other*, with no way out
    Note: from *c* the flow can go back to *a › a 1* when "up" happens (only if it can retry), so this part can repeat.
    Note: from *c* the flow can go back to *b* when "up" happens, so this part can repeat.
    Note: from *b* the flow can go back to *a › a 1* when the flow moves on by itself, so this part can repeat.

Health
  - [dead-end] *other* is a dead end: it is not an end state, yet nothing can move the flow out of it.
  - [no-fallback] In *b*, a failure of its service only applies under a condition (it is fatal) and there is no fallback, so if none holds, nothing happens.
